Learning Intelligence System and Health Score

2026 A course quality system that reads what learners write, not only the numbers they click.

The problem

Single-number satisfaction scores hid why a course worked or did not. Thousands of written comments sat unread.

What I built

  • A composite Health Score from 0 to 100 per course, weighted across five components, with 80% driven by comment analysis.
  • A 21-theme classification model (8 success themes, 13 improvement themes) that classified 99.1% of 16,620 comments across 150 courses.
  • Three interactive dashboards: a course-level report card, a seven-analysis insights view with drill-downs, and a sentiment trend report.
  • A Python quality tool that samples comments, detects keyword collisions, and produces sheets for human review.

What came out of it

  • A documented Course Quality Assurance Standard, a Health Score explainer for non-technical readers, and a build runbook.
  • A four-tier coaching and escalation protocol for instructors, from awareness through coaching and formal review.
  • Recalibrated grade bands and an evidence package aligned to industry award criteria, delivered at no added software cost.
  • Python
  • HTML dashboards
  • Qualitative analysis
  • Kirkpatrick and LTEM
  • Quality assurance

Course evaluation analytics and executive reporting

2025 to 2026 Raw LMS data turned into reports a CEO and board can read in minutes.

What I built

  • An 18-month evaluation synthesis report in 12 sections, calculated from complete datasets with no sampling.
  • A one-page executive summary with side-by-side 2025 and 2026 results.
  • An interactive Excel dashboard of Q1 2026 feedback (39,594 responses across 82 courses) with live filters and a short video walkthrough.
  • Evaluation instruments redesigned for on-demand, virtual, and in-person courses to meet NASBA and AICPA standards, with the survey trimmed from 11 questions to 9.

Reporting pipeline from LMS to dashboard

2025 to 2026 A repeatable data flow so quarterly reporting no longer starts from scratch.

What I built

  • Pulled survey, attendance, product, and instructor data from the Cadmium Elevate API using Postman.
  • Standardized field names, converted Likert scales from 0 to 4 into 1 to 5, and used a unique access ID to join complete response sets and align instructor ratings.
  • Documented the process with swimlane diagrams and three reusable playbooks for joining data, writing evaluation reports, and building dashboards.

Why it matters

Anyone on the team can follow the same steps and get the same answer. It also made it possible to run SCORM diagnostics across the catalog and write troubleshooting guides covering 200+ tracking fields.

How I work

I treat education as something to be measured and improved, with the same discipline as any other product.

Start from the behavior that needs to change

I begin with what learners need to do differently, then pick the format. That has meant instructor-led sessions, SCORM courses, short video, job aids, and interactive HTML, sometimes together.

Measure what learners say, in their own words

Scores tell you where to look. Comments tell you what to fix. I build the analysis so both reach decision makers in a form they can use.

Make it reusable

Playbooks, templates, runbooks, and documented pipelines mean the next course, report, or video starts further along. I also coach subject matter experts on writing objectives and designing for adult learners.

Work across teams

I partner with subject matter experts, marketing, technical teams, vendors, and executives, and I write the business cases that get the work approved.
